The Decision

Make Subtraction
Earn Its Place.

Compare remove, merge, hide and defer against a credible keep-or-clarify option. There is no reduction quota. Preserve the job, even when its surface changes.

Map the Jobs

Separate frequency from criticality. Low usage alone is not evidence of low value.

02

Count the Costs

03

Keep a Way Back

What Stays Available?

Latest, recent and pinned outputs; authorized export; comparison; safe retry. Existing links and keyboard paths need verification.

What Could Get Worse?

Consolidation may add navigation, disrupt familiar entry points or make side-by-side comparison harder. A grouped set of existing links is a conservative alternative.

What Would Change the Decision?

A lost critical task, unknown integration consumer or authorization regression blocks confident removal.
